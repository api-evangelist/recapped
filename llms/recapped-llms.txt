# RecappED

> RecappED (recapped.io) is an AI-powered digital sales room and customer onboarding platform that consolidates buyer and seller activity — mutual action plans, deal management, buyer-engagement tracking, and sales-to-implementation handoffs — into a single collaborative workspace. The product does not publish a public REST API or developer portal; integrations are delivered through native connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Gong, Clari) and Zapier. Note: the platform announced it is shutting down on 2026-07-31 and is not accepting new customers.
