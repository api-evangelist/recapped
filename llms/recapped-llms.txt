# RecappED

> RecappED (recapped.io) was an AI-powered digital sales room and customer onboarding platform for B2B revenue teams — mutual action plans, deal management, buyer-engagement tracking, AI deal scoring and coaching, and sales-to-implementation handoffs in one collaborative workspace. RecappED published no public REST API, no OpenAPI, no developer portal and no MCP or A2A surface; programmatic connectivity was sold as native connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Gong, Zoom) and a Zapier integration. The company announced it is shutting down on 2026-07-31 and is not taking on new customers; the site and application were still serving as of 2026-08-13.

## Status
- Shutdown announced sitewide: "Unfortunately, after 7 long years Recapped is shutting down 7/31/2026 and will not be taking on new customers." (banner on every page of https://www.recapped.io/)
- Not accepting new customers. Pricing tiers are still rendered but are not purchasable.

- [Pricing](https://www.recapped.io/pricing): Per-seat and flat-monthly tiers; no API or developer tier.
- [Integrations](https://www.recapped.io/integrations): Native connectors and Zapier automation.
- [Workflow automations](https://www.recapped.io/workflows): No-code workflow automations.

- [Zapier integration](https://www.recapped.io/features/zapier-integration): Marketed connectivity to 7,000+ apps. No public Zapier app listing exists (zapier.com/apps/recapped* returns 404).
- [Templates](https://www.recapped.io/templates): Sales room and mutual action plan template library.
- [Request a demo](https://www.recapped.io/demo): Product demo request.
- [Login / free account](https://app.recapped.io): Application sign-in and sign-up.

## Developer surface
There is none. Probed 2026-08-13: no OpenAPI/Swagger at any location on www.recapped.io or app.recapped.io; api.recapped.io, docs.recapped.io and developer(s).recapped.io have no DNS record; every /.well-known/ path 404s on www.recapped.io and returns an HTML SPA shell on app.recapped.io; no /llms.txt (404); no MCP server; no A2A agent card; no GraphQL endpoint; no published SDK on npm, PyPI, RubyGems or NuGet.

- [GitHub organization](https://github.com/Recapped): Two public repositories.
- [Customer offboarding docs](https://github.com/Recapped/customer-offboarding-docs): The one machine-oriented document RecappED publishes — the schema of the customer data export (workspace_meta_data, tabs_data, blocks_data, tasks_data, comments_data), the join keys, and the procedure for reconstructing a workspace from the exported reports.
